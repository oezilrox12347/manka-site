Manka: Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026. Applies to the Manka app for Android.

The short version

Manka collects nothing. It has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not request Android's internet permission, so it has no way to send information off your phone.

What Manka stores on your phone

These stay in the app's private storage on your device and are never transmitted to us or anyone else:

  • Your practice history: when you counted, how many beads, the pace, and how many times you paused and returned.
  • The name you choose to give Orbi, if any.
  • Your answers to the optional daily "weather" check-in (clear, cloudy, foggy, stormy) and whether you said it was clearer afterwards. This is a self-reported mood note.
  • Any short notes you choose to keep in "Nine thanks".
  • Your reminders and settings (bead material, place, sound, haptic strength).

What we collect, share or sell

Nothing. We receive no personal data, usage data, device identifiers or crash reports from the app, so there is nothing to share or sell.

Permissions

  • Vibration: for the bead clicks.
  • Notifications: requested only if you set a reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no server is involved.
  • Run at startup: so your reminders are restored after the phone restarts.

Backups and moving phones

Manka is excluded from Android's cloud backup, so no copy of your data is stored in your Google account. If you set up a new phone using Android's direct phone-to-phone transfer (Android 12 or later), your Manka data can be copied straight to the new phone. We never see it.

Deleting your data

In the app, open You, then My strands, then Clear. Uninstalling Manka removes everything it stored. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Manka is intended for adults. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Not medical care

Manka is a practice aid for breathing and counting. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any medical or mental-health condition. If you are struggling, please speak to a qualified professional.

Changes

If a future version changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date will change.
